90 mins · £200 · First Visit

Initial Consultation

Ninety minutes gives us unpressured space to hear your full story, see how things move comfortably, and begin gentle hands-on care on the same visit.

  • Your story first
    We talk through what brought you in, your daily routine, and what you would like to feel easier doing.
  • Gentle movement check
    Simple, everyday movements in loose clothing help spot where things feel guarded or stiff.
  • Tailored treatment & plan
    Supportive hands-on care is explained as we go, followed by practical ideas for between visits.

45 mins · £100 · Ongoing Care

Follow-Up Session

Returning sessions build smoothly on what worked last time, adjusting treatment as your comfort and movement shift over the weeks.

  • How things have settled
    We review what has eased, note anything still tender, and see how your week has been.
  • Focused hands-on care
    Attentive manual treatment is adjusted to how your body feels on the day.
  • Everyday support
    You leave with realistic posture, breathing, or gentle mobility habits for work and home.

Step by step

What happens in a session

Every appointment follows the same clear rhythm, so you always know what comes next.

01 · Conversation

An unhurried conversation

We start sitting down and listening — your routine, what feels tight or sore, and what you would like to get back to doing comfortably.

02 · Treatment

Gentle, explained treatment

Soft tissue release, joint mobilisation, and gentle stretching — always described beforehand and adjusted to your comfort.

03 · Aftercare

Clear aftercare & next steps

You leave with straightforward self-care ideas, a realistic movement tip if helpful, and honest guidance on whether you need a follow-up.
