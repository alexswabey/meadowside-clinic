90 mins · First Visit

Initial Consultation

Your first appointment gives us generous time to listen properly, understand your daily routine, and begin gentle treatment at a pace that feels comfortable for you.

  • Conversation first — we talk through what brought you in, your general wellbeing, and what you hope to feel easier doing.
  • Gentle movement check — simple, comfortable movements to notice where things feel tight or restricted.
  • Tailored hands-on care — gentle osteopathic techniques explained step by step, plus clear ideas to support yourself between visits.

45 mins · Ongoing Care

Follow-Up Session

Returning visits focus on how you have felt since your last appointment, adjusting treatment gently around how your body responds over time.

  • Progress check-in — reviewing what eased, what still feels tender, and any changes in your week.
  • Focused hands-on treatment — unhurried manual care paced to your comfort on the day.
  • Practical everyday guidance — simple pacing, breathing, or gentle mobility ideas for home and work.
