Understanding the Modality

More than just the head: whole-body osteopathy

Cranial osteopathy is not a separate profession—it is a refined, gentle approach practised by registered osteopaths. Using light, steady contact at the head, spine, ribcage, pelvis, or sacrum, Lydia assesses areas of muscular guarding, joint stiffness, and everyday postural strain.

Sessions are calm, unhurried, and carried out fully clothed. Visitors commonly seek care for non-urgent neck and shoulder tightness, lower-back ache, jaw tension, postural adaptation during pregnancy, or gentle settling support for babies and children.

Frequently Asked Questions

Cranial osteopathy is very gentle and non-forceful. Many patients describe the sensation as light pressure, warmth, or deep relaxation. Lydia does not use forceful joint manipulation unless discussed and appropriate for you, and always works within your comfort.

No GP referral is required to book an osteopathy appointment. However, if you have a complex medical history or Lydia notices anything that would benefit from your doctor's input, she will always recommend speaking with your GP.

Comfortable, loose-fitting clothing such as soft trousers, leggings, or a t-shirt works well. Because cranial osteopathy uses very light contact, treatment can usually take place fully clothed.

Appointments for babies and children are scheduled for 45 minutes specifically so there is never any rush. You are welcome to feed, change, or cuddle your baby at any point, and Lydia can often treat gently while your baby is held in your arms.

Everyone responds differently depending on how long an issue has been present and their daily routine. At the end of your first session, Lydia will share an honest view of what she recommends, and progress is reviewed after each visit.

Meadowside Clinic does not operate drop-in hours so that every patient has quiet, uninterrupted time. Simply send an enquiry via the Contact page and Lydia will offer available days and times that suit you.
