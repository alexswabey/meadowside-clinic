Practice Story

Why Lydia founded Meadowside Clinic

Lydia established Meadowside Clinic near Twickenham Green to offer a calmer alternative to high-turnover appointments. After completing her four-year Master of Osteopathy degree in London and practising within multidisciplinary clinics across South West London, she saw how much patients valued having time to be properly heard before hands-on care begins.

Alongside structural and soft-tissue osteopathy, Lydia completed dedicated postgraduate study in cranial osteopathy. Cranial osteopathy is a refined, gentle branch of osteopathic care that uses light manual contact to feel for subtle patterns of tension and muscular guarding throughout the whole body—not only the head, as the name sometimes suggests.

Because the contact is calm and non-forceful, many people find it reassuring when they feel sore, fatigued, expectant, or hesitant about stronger joint manipulation. Lydia explains what she notices in plain English, checks comfort at every stage, and shares realistic home advice to support everyday ease.

Standards you can rely on

In the UK, the title “osteopath” is protected by statute under the Osteopaths Act 1993. Every practising osteopath must be registered with the General Osteopathic Council (GOsC), adhere to the Osteopathic Practice Standards, hold full professional indemnity insurance, and complete verified continuing professional development each year.

Clear clinical boundaries: Osteopathy complements conventional medical care rather than replacing it. Where symptoms fall outside osteopathic scope or would benefit from GP investigation, Lydia will always signpost you promptly and responsibly.

Clinical Philosophy

Three principles behind every session

01

Listening before treating

Every appointment starts with an unhurried conversation about your symptoms, daily routines, and health history so care is grounded in context.

02

Whole-person perspective

A stiff neck or aching lower back rarely exists in isolation; Lydia considers how breathing, posture, rib movement, and everyday habits interact.

03

Respectful, gentle touch

Treatment works with your tissues rather than forcing them, making sessions suitable for sensitive adults, expectant mothers, babies, and older visitors.
