A quiet clinic built around listening first

I opened Meadowside Clinic near Twickenham Green so local residents have a calm, one-to-one place to be heard, understand what their body is telling them, and move forward comfortably.

About image slot (16:9, eager): Environmental scene of Lydia welcoming or talking calmly in the sunlit consultation space.

How we work together

Every person arrives with a different daily routine—long hours at a desk, physically demanding hobbies, pregnancy changes, or simply wanting to stay comfortable as the years pass.

Rather than rushing to a fixed routine, sessions adapt quietly to how you feel on the day, with clear explanations and your comfort checked at every stage.

What shapes each visit

  • Unhurried conversation — plenty of space to ask questions without feeling rushed out the door.
  • Collaborative comfort — every movement test or gentle hands-on technique is explained and agreed together.
  • Continuity of care — you see Lydia directly at every appointment, from first visit onward.

In patients’ words

What visitors mention most

  • Feeling genuinely listened to before any hands-on care
  • Calm, natural-light room without busy waiting areas
  • Plain-English guidance they can actually use at home
