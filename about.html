Care philosophy

How we work together

Every person arrives with a different daily routine—long hours at a desk, physically demanding hobbies, pregnancy changes, or staying comfortable as the years pass. Sessions adapt quietly to how you feel on the day, with clear explanations and your comfort checked at every stage.

01 · Listen

Unhurried conversation

Plenty of space to ask questions and explain how things feel without feeling rushed out the door.

02 · Agree

Collaborative comfort

Every movement test or gentle hands-on technique is explained clearly and agreed together first.

03 · Continuity

One practitioner throughout

You see Lydia directly at every appointment, from your first hello through ongoing follow-ups.
