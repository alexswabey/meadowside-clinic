A thoughtful, unhurried approach to osteopathy

Lydia founded Meadowside Clinic near Twickenham Green to create a quiet, reassuring space where patients never feel rushed through a short appointment slot. After completing her four-year Master of Osteopathy degree in London and working within established multidisciplinary clinics across South West London, she opened her own Twickenham practice to offer dedicated one-to-one care.

Alongside structural osteopathic skills, Lydia pursued postgraduate study in cranial osteopathy. Cranial osteopathy is a refined, gentle branch of osteopathy that uses very light manual contact to feel for subtle tension patterns throughout the whole body—not only the head, as the name sometimes suggests.

Because the touch is calm and non-invasive, many people find it particularly reassuring when they feel sensitive, fatigued, or unsure about stronger manual techniques. Lydia works closely with each patient to explain what she observes, check comfort at every step, and support everyday movement and wellbeing.

Standards you can rely on

In the UK, the title “osteopath” is protected by law. Every practising osteopath must be registered with the General Osteopathic Council (GOsC), meet rigorous clinical and ethical standards, carry professional indemnity insurance, and complete ongoing professional development each year.

Clear clinical boundaries: Osteopathy is complementary to conventional medical care. Where an issue falls outside osteopathic scope or requires GP assessment, Lydia will always signpost you promptly and appropriately.
