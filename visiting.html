Arriving Early or With a Buggy

Please arrive no more than 5–10 minutes before your scheduled time. There is sheltered ground-floor space inside the entrance hall for prams, pushchairs, or folding mobility aids.

Getting Here

Public transport & local parking

01

By Bus

Routes 110, 290, and 490 stop directly alongside Twickenham Green on Staines Road (A311)—roughly a level 2-minute walk to the entrance of Meadowside Mews.

02

By Train

Strawberry Hill Station is a level 10-minute walk south-east via Wellesley Road and Hampton Road. Twickenham Station is around 12 minutes north-east along Heath Road.

03

By Car & Parking

Short-stay pay-and-display bays are located along Staines Road and around Twickenham Green (2–3 minutes' walk). Neighbouring side streets operate daytime Controlled Parking Zones—please check street signage.

Step-Free Ground-Floor Studio

Physical & sensory accessibility

Meadowside Clinic is designed so older visitors, wheelchair or walking-frame users, and parents with pushchairs can visit comfortably without stairs or cramped corridors.

Entrance

Step-free threshold

Level paving leads 20 metres from the drop-off point on Staines Road to a flush, step-free ground-floor front door.

Interior Space

Wide doorways & seating

Generous turning room for wheelchairs and buggies, plus firm, supportive armchairs with armrests in the waiting area.

Facilities

Accessible ground-floor WC

Step-free restroom with support rails and baby-changing space located directly adjacent to the waiting room.

Atmosphere

Quiet sensory environment

Natural daylight, no background music or harsh fluorescent lighting, and buffer time between appointments so the room stays calm.
