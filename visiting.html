Getting here & step-free access

Choose how you are travelling for straightforward local directions.

Walkable from two local stations

The clinic at 14 The Green is roughly an 8-minute level walk from Strawberry Hill Station and around 12 minutes along Heath Road from Twickenham Station. Local buses stop right beside Twickenham Green, less than two minutes on foot from the clinic door.

  • Strawberry Hill: ~8 min level walk
  • Twickenham Station: ~12 min via Heath Rd
  • Bus routes: 110, 290, and 490 at The Green

Drop-off & local parking

Short-stay pay-and-display bays sit along the edges of Twickenham Green and nearby side roads, within a two-minute flat walk of the clinic door. If someone is dropping you off, Staines Road along the northern edge offers an easy, level spot to step out.

  • Street bays: Along Twickenham Green edges
  • Drop-off: Level kerb on Staines Road side
  • Distance: Under 2 mins on flat pavement

Ground-floor, step-free clinic

Both the level pavement entrance and our treatment room are on the ground floor with wide doorways and no steps—comfortable for buggies, crutches, or anyone preferring step-free entry. Feel free to mention any seating or arrival preferences when getting in touch.

  • Entrance: Flush threshold from pavement
  • Interior: Ground-floor treatment & WC
  • Buggies & mobility: Wide clear corridor

What arriving feels like

Aiming to arrive five minutes before your appointment is plenty—there is calm ground-floor seating waiting inside, with generous gaps between patients so the space stays quiet.
