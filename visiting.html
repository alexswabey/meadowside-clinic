Buses & Trains

  • Buses: Routes 110, 290, and 490 stop directly alongside Twickenham Green on Staines Road, roughly 2 minutes on foot from the clinic.
  • Strawberry Hill Station: Approximately a 10-minute level walk south-east via Wellesley Road and Hampton Road.
  • Twickenham Station: Approximately a 12-minute walk north-east along Heath Road and London Road.

Arriving by Car

Parking Advice

While Meadowside Mews itself is pedestrian-friendly with no general patient parking, there are several convenient options close by:

  • Pay-and-display bays: Short-stay bays are available along Staines Road and around Twickenham Green (typically 2–3 minutes' walk).
  • Residential permit zones: Neighbouring streets operate Controlled Parking Zones during set daytime hours—please check street signage before leaving your vehicle.
  • Drop-off access: Patients with reduced mobility can be dropped off at the entrance to Meadowside Mews, roughly 20 metres on flat paving from the clinic door.

Step-Free Clinic

Physical Accessibility

Meadowside Clinic is designed to be welcoming and straightforward for visitors with mobility aids, pushchairs, or sensory sensitivities:

  • Step-free entrance: Ground-floor access throughout with no steps at the front threshold.
  • Wide doorways & seating: Space for wheelchairs, walking frames, and buggies inside the reception area and treatment room.
  • Ground-floor WC: Accessible toilet facilities with support rails located next to the waiting space.
  • Calm environment: Appointments are spaced out so the waiting area stays quiet and unhurried. Please note any access preferences when sending your enquiry.
